### [7:05-8:25] VALUE BEAT 5 - "How do you know the number's right before the board sees it?" CLAIM (7:05-7:17): "Automation with no check just produces the wrong number faster. So the last piece is a check that catches it before a person does." (MODALITY: talking head)

PROOF / DEMO (7:17-8:15): A high-level screen-record of the check, then a data-viz of it firing. (MODALITY: screen-record into data-viz) - SCREEN-RECORD (MODALITY: screen-record): show a small check on the report that compares the automated total against a control figure pulled straight from the source. It reads green when the two tie out and shows the exact gap when they don't. Keep it simple and buyer-level, one green card, one amber card. - DATA-VIZ (MODALITY switch: data-viz): animate the check firing. Two totals slide together. When they match, a green tick. When one is off, the card flips to amber and shows the exact gap, so the report tells you before the board does.

PAYOFF (8:15-8:25): "Ask for one reconciliation check that ties the final number back to a control total from the source. If it doesn't tie, the report flags it. Not the board, not an angry email the next morning. The report itself, before the pack goes out. That's what lets you sign off with your name on it." (ON-SCREEN: "TAKEAWAY 5 - Add one check that ties the final number back to the source. The report flags itself before the board does.")
