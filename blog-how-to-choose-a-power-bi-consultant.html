How to Choose a Power BI Consultant Without Getting Burned by a Pretty Demo

Quick answer. Here is how to choose a power bi consultant: look at the data model behind their past reports, ask for a model-view screenshot, buy a paid scoping day, and watch how they define one shared metric like net revenue. Confirm who owns refresh after handoff. Day rates run about 700 to 1,800 euros. ---

Most teams get how to choose a power bi consultant wrong, and it shows up in a board meeting three months later when two dashboards pull the same revenue line and land on different numbers. The mistake is judging the candidate by the dashboard they demo. That dashboard is the easy 10 percent of the job. The parts that decide whether your reports still work next quarter sit underneath it, in a place a sales demo never goes.

This is a buyer's guide. It covers why these hires go sideways, what a strong power bi consultant does behind the report, the questions to ask before you sign, and what the work should cost in 2026.

Why do most power bi consultant hires go sideways?

This plays out in real board meetings. The head of sales shows a slide: last month brought in 4.1 million euros. The finance lead has 3.8 million on the same line. Both numbers came out of Power BI. Both look right inside the report that made them.

Here is the mechanism. The two reports were built on separate imports of the same source data, and the monthly total was written a different way in each file. One counts credit notes, the other skips them. Nothing crashed. The number is just defined twice, so it lands twice.

Most Power BI files start life as a copy of a spreadsheet, and about 94 percent of business spreadsheets used in decision-making contain errors (Poon et al., 2024). Hire someone who imports that spreadsheet and recolors the charts, and the errors slide straight into the report with a nicer font on top. That is how a company joins the 67 percent of organizations that do not fully trust the data they use to make decisions (Precisely and Drexel LeBow, 2025). The chart got prettier and the trust got worse.

What does a good power bi consultant do behind the report?

The visuals are the last thing a strong consultant touches. Here is where the real hours go, and what each part controls.

  • The data model. They shape your tables into a star schema: one fact table for events like sales, plus small dimension tables for products, dates, and regions. This is what lets one date filter drive every chart on the page at once.
  • DAX measures. They write each metric one time, so net revenue means the same thing on every report. One definition, one number, everywhere.
  • Power Query. They clean the incoming data (fixing date formats, splitting columns, dropping duplicate rows) before it reaches a single chart.
  • Incremental refresh. For a large sales table they set RangeStart and RangeEnd so Power BI reloads only the last few days each morning instead of ten years of history. A refresh that ran for two hours drops to a couple of minutes.
  • Row-level security. They set roles so the Berlin sales lead sees Berlin rows and no one else's, then confirm it using View As before handoff.
  • Deployment pipelines. They move a report from development to test to production without hand-editing the live copy, so a Tuesday fix does not knock over the Wednesday board pack.

None of that appears in a sales demo. All of it decides whether the report still works in month six.

How to choose a power bi consultant, step by step

You do not need to read DAX to run a sharp process. Do these five things, in order.

  1. Name the one report that hurts. Write a single sentence: "Our monthly sales pack takes a day to reconcile and two people disagree on the total." That scopes the job better than any feature list.
  2. Ask for a model-view screenshot from real past work. You are checking for a clean star schema with a few linked tables, not judging the design.
  3. Buy a paid scoping day before any large commitment. Give read-only access to real data and ask the candidate to define one metric with you, live. You learn more in that day than across three sales calls.
  4. Ask who owns refresh after handoff. A report nobody can refresh in month two is a slow way to lose the fee you paid.
  5. Get the model documented. A short note on the tables, the main measures, and the security roles means the next person does not start from zero.

Run that and the gap between a real consultant and a report decorator shows up fast, usually on the scoping day, long before it can cost you a board meeting.

What should you ask before you sign?

Two questions separate the two kinds of candidate. Ask both, and watch the answer instead of the confidence.

First: "Show me the model view from a past project, not the dashboard." A serious consultant pulls up a clean star schema and walks you through the tables. A decorator shows one giant flat table or changes the subject.

Second: "How would you define net revenue so every report reads the same?" A real answer names credit notes, returns, and a date table. A weak answer is "we will build a visual for it."

What does a power bi consultant cost, and what changes the price?

Rates depend on scope and seniority, so treat these as planning ranges for 2026, not a quote. Freelance day rates usually run 700 to 1,200 euros. A senior specialist or a small agency runs 1,200 to 1,800 euros a day. A fixed-scope project, meaning one clean model plus three reports with refresh and security done right, tends to land in the low tens of thousands.

Keep two bills separate in your head. The consultant's fee is the build. The Power BI Pro license (about 10 euros per user each month) is what Microsoft charges to publish and view reports. When you compare power bi consulting cost across quotes, make sure each one covers the same scope. A low day rate often hides a long change-fee list later, so a cheap quote can end up the expensive one.

The spend pays back when the model is right. Insights-driven companies are 8.5 times more likely to report 20 percent or more in yearly revenue growth (Forrester). A good power bi consulting engagement is really buying that: reports several teams can act on without arguing about the total first.

How do you check the work is solid after handoff?

You can confirm a good hire without opening the report editor. Check three things a month after the consultant leaves.

The refresh runs on its own schedule and finishes on time. Two teams pulling the same metric get the same number. Row-level security shows each person only their own rows, which you test by logging in as a regular user.

If all three are true, you chose well. If the refresh needs a manual nudge every morning, or the totals drift from team to team, the model underneath still needs work. That is the conversation to have before the retainer renews, while you still have the person who built it.

Frequently asked questions

How is a power bi consultant different from a general bi consultant? A power bi consultant works hands-on inside the tool: model, DAX, refresh, security, and reports. A general bi consultant may cover strategy, other platforms, and a wider team. You want the hands-on skills either way, so ask who writes the DAX on your project.

Do I need a consultant, or can my in-house analyst handle it? If your analyst already builds star schemas and writes clean DAX, a short review may be all you need. If your reports sit in one flat table and refresh slowly, paying a consultant to rebuild the model is usually cheaper than another year of manual reconciliation.

How long does a Power BI project take? A single-report rebuild with a proper model, refresh, and security is often four to eight weeks. A platform serving several teams runs longer. The data cleanup sets the timeline, not the charts.

Does the fee include Power BI licenses? No. The fee covers the build. Power BI Pro is a separate Microsoft charge, roughly 10 euros per user each month, for publishing and viewing. Confirm the license count during scoping so it does not surprise you at launch.

What is the clearest sign I picked the wrong consultant? A beautiful dashboard handed over with no documentation and a model only they understand. When they leave, the reports drift and nobody can trace why. Ask for the documented model up front so this never becomes your problem.
