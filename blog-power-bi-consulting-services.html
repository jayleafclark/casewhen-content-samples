Power BI Consulting Services vs. Big Consultancies: What's Actually Different

Quick answer. Power BI consulting services cover the work of turning raw data into reports people trust: data modeling, DAX measures, refresh schedules, row-level security, and training. A specialist does this for a fixed scope in weeks. A large firm wraps the same build into a bigger program with account managers and much higher day rates. ---

Most teams buy Power BI consulting services expecting a finished dashboard, and the trouble shows up about three months later, when the Monday refresh runs for two hours and two people in the same meeting read out different revenue numbers. The dashboard was the easy part. What you really pay a good partner for is the plumbing underneath it: the data model, the measures, the refresh schedule, and who is allowed to see which rows.

These services include far more than the dashboard. A large consultancy can charge much more for the identical build, and a hands-on specialist looks nothing like a firm that mostly sells you hours.

What do Power BI consulting services actually include?

The charts are the last 10% of the job. Most of the paid hours go into the parts that decide whether the report still works next quarter.

  • Data model. A good partner shapes your tables into a star schema: one fact table for events like sales, plus small dimension tables for products, dates, and regions. That structure lets a single filter drive every visual on the page at once.
  • DAX measures. Each metric gets written once, as a measure, so "revenue" returns the same value on every page. One definition, one answer.
  • Power Query. They clean the incoming data (fixing date formats, splitting columns, dropping duplicate rows) before it reaches a single chart.
  • Incremental refresh. For a large sales table they set RangeStart and RangeEnd so Power BI reloads only the last few days each morning instead of ten years of history. That two-hour refresh drops to a couple of minutes.
  • Row-level security. They build roles so the Berlin sales lead sees only Berlin rows, and they prove it with the View As button before handoff.
  • Deployment pipelines. They move a report from development to test to production without hand-editing the live copy, so a Tuesday change doesn't break the Wednesday board pack.

None of that shows up in a slick demo. All of it is what you are really buying when you buy Power BI consulting services.

Why does a big consultancy charge three times more for the same Power BI work?

Walk into a large firm and the day rate often starts near 1,800 euros and climbs from there. Some of that buys real value: a second reviewer on your model, written process, and backup when someone is away sick. A lot of it pays for layers you never touch, like the account manager, the engagement partner, and the sales team that won the deal.

Here is the part that catches finance teams off guard. On a big-firm project the person in the pitch is often a senior, and the person writing your DAX is often a junior two years out of university. The senior name sits on the invoice. Ask directly who does the hands-on build, and get the answer in writing.

The math still has to pay back. Poor data quality costs organizations an average of 12.9 million dollars a year (Gartner), and 67% of organizations don't fully trust the data they use to make decisions (Precisely and Drexel LeBow, 2025). A rebuild that fixes those two problems is worth real money. Paying triple for the account management wrapped around the same rebuild is a much harder case to make.

How do you tell a Power BI specialist from a firm that resells hours?

Ask to see a model view from past work, not the finished dashboard. A specialist shows you a clean star schema with a few connected tables. A reseller shows one giant flat table, or steers you back to screenshots of pretty charts.

Ask how they would define one shared measure, say net revenue, so every report reads the same. A strong answer names credit notes, returns, and a date table. A weak answer sounds like "we'll add a visual for that."

This is the point where general business intelligence consulting and hands-on Power BI work separate. Strategy decks have their place, and business intelligence consulting services can help you plan a wider data program. But the number on your board slide is decided by the DAX, the model, and the refresh. Good bi consulting services put a senior on all three, and they can show you the model that proves it.

What does a good first engagement look like?

You don't need to read DAX to run a sharp process. Do these five things in order.

  1. Name the one report that hurts. Say which one, in a sentence. "Our monthly sales pack takes a full day to reconcile and two people disagree on the total." That single line scopes the job better than any feature list.
  2. Buy a paid scoping day before any big commitment. Give read-only access to real data and watch how the partner works before you sign a project.
  3. Give them one metric to define with you. Ask them to write net revenue as a DAX measure on your own data. You learn more in that one exercise than in three sales calls.
  4. Agree who owns refresh after handoff. A report nobody can refresh in month two is a slow way to waste the fee. Write down the owner and the schedule.
  5. Get the model documented. Ask for a short note on the tables, the main measures, and the security roles, so the next person doesn't start from zero.

Run that process and the gap between a real specialist and a report decorator shows up fast, usually on the scoping day, long before it can cost you a board meeting.

How much should Power BI consulting services cost?

Rates depend on scope and seniority, so treat these as planning ranges for 2026, not a quote. Keep one thing separate in your head: the consulting fee is the build. The Power BI Pro license (about 10 euros per user each month) is what Microsoft charges to publish and view. Those are two different bills.

Frequently asked questions

Are Power BI consulting services the same as business intelligence consulting? They overlap. Business intelligence consulting can cover strategy, other tools, and a wider data program. Power BI consulting services are the hands-on build inside Power BI: model, measures, refresh, security, and reports. Most teams need the hands-on skills either way, so ask who writes the DAX.

How long does a first project usually take? A single-report rebuild with a proper data model, refresh, and row-level security is often four to eight weeks. A platform serving several teams runs longer. The data cleanup, not the charts, sets the timeline.

Do the fees include Power BI licenses? No. The fee covers the build. Power BI Pro is a separate Microsoft charge (roughly 10 euros per user each month) for publishing and viewing. Confirm your license count during scoping so it doesn't surprise you at launch.

Is a boutique team really better than a large consultancy? For a focused rebuild, a small senior team is often better value, because the person you meet is the person who does the work. For a huge program across many departments, a large firm's bench and process can earn the higher rate. Match the size of the partner to the size of the problem.

How do I know the work is good after they leave? Check three things a month later: the refresh runs on schedule on its own, two teams pulling the same metric get the same number, and row-level security shows each person only their rows. If all three are true, you hired well.
