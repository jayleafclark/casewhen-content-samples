This video breaks down Power BI Pro cost against Premium Per User and Microsoft Fabric F64 capacity in real numbers. Power BI Pro is $14 per user per month for builders and viewers. Premium Per User is $24 and adds larger models, more refreshes, paginated reports and deployment pipelines for a small heavy team. Fabric capacity, the F64 tier that replaced Premium P1, is a flat cost of around $5,000 a month on a reservation and gives viewers free access. The break-even is your capacity price divided by your per-seat price: roughly 360 viewers for reserved F64, 600 on pay-as-you-go, and about 208 users when you compare against PPU. Fabric SKUs below F64 still need a Pro seat per viewer, so a cheap capacity does not remove per-user licensing. Once you are on capacity, a one-year reservation cuts about 40 percent off pay-as-you-go, and switching the capacity off outside working hours means you only pay for the hours people use it.
