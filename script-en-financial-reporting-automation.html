### [5:35-7:05] VALUE BEAT 4 - "How do you get one number finance agrees on?" CLAIM (5:35-5:45): "Every figure in every report should come from one measure, defined one time." (MODALITY: custom animation - star schema and the single measure)

(ON-SCREEN: "TAKEAWAY 4 - One measure per figure, defined once, so every report agrees")

### [7:05-8:35] VALUE BEAT 5 - "Why is the refresh still slow, and how do you fix it?" (pays off the open loop) CLAIM (7:05-7:15): "By default, every refresh reloads your entire history. You only ever need the newest month." (MODALITY: custom animation - incremental refresh partitions)

PROOF / DEMO (7:15-8:18): - CUSTOM ANIMATION SPEC (incremental refresh): Draw the dataset as a row of date partitions, one tile per month, going back several years (Jan, Feb, Mar ... this month). On a normal refresh, every tile lights up and reprocesses at once, and a timer in the corner climbs and turns amber as it drags on. Now switch on incremental refresh: all the old tiles go cold grey and sit still, and only the current-month tile reprocesses in the accent colour. The timer drops back to a few seconds. - SCREEN-RECORD (MODALITY micro-switch: screen-record): show the incremental refresh policy panel in Power BI, set to archive three years and refresh only the last one month. Point at the two sliders so the viewer can copy it exactly.

PAYOFF (8:18-8:30): "Turn on incremental refresh so only recent data reloads. A close that used to reprocess years of history every single night stops doing that, and your reports come back in seconds. That's the setting most teams never find." (ON-SCREEN: "TAKEAWAY 5 - Turn on incremental refresh so only new data reloads")

RE-HOOK (8:30-8:35): "Fast and correct now. There's one step left before you let finance anywhere near it."

### [8:35-10:05] VALUE BEAT 6 - "How do you trust it before you hit send?" CLAIM (8:35-8:45): "Automation with no check just produces the wrong number faster." (MODALITY: screen-record - the reconciliation check)

PAYOFF (9:48-10:00): "Add one reconciliation check that ties the automated output back to a control total from the source. If it doesn't tie, the report tells you. Not finance, not the board, the report itself, before the pack goes out." (ON-SCREEN: "TAKEAWAY 6 - Add a reconciliation check that ties back to a source control total")
