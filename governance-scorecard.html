What is inside your report

Ten questions, four areas that decide whether Fabric goes smoothly

Most teams move to Fabric and only find the governance holes months later, once reports are wrong and nobody can say why. This scorecard checks the four things that cause that, and hands you a plain report you can act on the same day.

01

Workspace structure

We check how your workspaces are split across dev, test and production, who owns what, and whether naming will still make sense once you have thirty of them instead of three.

02

Row-level security and access

We look at how people get access, whether row-level security is defined once and reused, and where a manager could quietly see numbers they shouldn't.

03

OneLake and medallion architecture

We check whether your bronze, silver and gold layers are actually separated in OneLake, or whether raw and cleaned data are mixed together in ways that get expensive to untangle later.

04

Lineage and trust

We look at whether you can trace a number in a report back to its source, and whether a change upstream will warn the people downstream before it breaks their dashboard.
