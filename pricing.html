Why these prices (the logic)
Free scorecard, email-only. An interactive assessment beats a static PDF, and a one-field form converts far better than a two-field one (about 4.4% vs 2.9%). We profile the lead after capture, not before.
€49 tripwire as a buyer filter. A small paid yes separates real buyers from browsers and pays for the traffic. It is tightly tied to the services so it scopes the next step, not a random product.
€299 course as the pipeline. It is both revenue and lead-gen. It teaches the three things buyers search for most, then the "now do it on your data" bridge invites the mid-ticket.
€1,500 mid-ticket scopes the high-ticket. Dashboard-in-a-Day on the client's own data is a live scoping session. It de-risks the retainer for both sides before anyone signs a big number.
US vs DACH framing differs. US and UK buyers respond to build-first, direct-to-call. DACH buyers respond to education and workshop-first, in German, anchored on modeling and governance.
AI is the trigger, not a line item. Copilot and Fabric AI only pay off on governed data, so the AI conversation feeds offers 2, 4 and 5. We never sell "AI consulting" as its own thing.
