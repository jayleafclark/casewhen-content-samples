Governance & trustENAustinpower bi incremental refresh
Your Power BI refresh doesn't need to reload 4 years of history every single morning.
That's the whole job of incremental refresh. It only reprocesses the partitions where data changed, so a 40-minute refresh drops to about two. The catch: it only kicks in if your Power Query folds. No folding, and Power BI incremental refresh silently reloads all of it while your settings say you're optimized. Check the query diagnostics before you trust the toggle.
