Day 8 · MonENAustinincremental refresh power bi
Your Power BI model reloads all five years of history on every refresh. It doesn't have to.
Most reports run a full reload because nobody turned on incremental refresh. Power BI can partition by date instead. You set two parameters, RangeStart and RangeEnd, tell it to keep five years and only reprocess the last 10 days. A 40-minute refresh drops to under a minute, and your capacity stops choking at 8am.

Day 21 · SunENAustinpower bi incremental refresh
Your Power BI refresh doesn't need to reload 4 years of history every single morning.
That's the whole job of incremental refresh. It only reprocesses the partitions where data changed, so a 40-minute refresh drops to about two. The catch: it only kicks in if your Power Query folds. No folding, and Power BI incremental refresh silently reloads all of it while your settings say you're optimized. Check the query diagnostics before you trust the toggle.
casewhen.co
SHIP ✓ gatedHook style: blunt myth-buster ('you don't need to X'). Spicy take is that plenty of incremental-refresh setups do nothing because the query doesn't fold, so teams think they're optimized while reloading everything.
