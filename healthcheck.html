What we actually check

A real diagnostic of one report, not a generic checklist

You pick the report that's embarrassing you. We open it up and look at the four things that cause almost every slow Power BI report. Here's what we go through.

1

A Performance Analyzer read

We run Performance Analyzer on your slowest visuals and see where the milliseconds actually go, whether it's a DAX query, the visual itself, or waiting on the model. You find out if it's one bad card or the whole page.

2

A data-model check

We look at whether your model is a proper star schema or a tangle of flat tables and bidirectional relationships. Most slow reports trace back to the model, and this is usually where the real fix lives.

3

The top DAX offenders

We pull out the specific measures that are doing the most damage, the ones with nested iterators or calculated columns that should be measures, and tell you which ones are worth rewriting first.

4

Refresh and gateway issues

If the pain is slow or failing refreshes, we check your gateway setup, refresh windows, and whether incremental refresh would help. You'll know if the problem is the report or the pipeline behind it.
