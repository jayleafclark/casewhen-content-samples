[0:45-2:15] VALUE BEAT 1: What does the PL-300 certification actually prove?

CLAIM: "The PL-300 proves you know the buttons, not that you can model real data."

(ON-SCREEN: claim card, "A cert proves you know the tool. Not that you can solve the problem.")

PROOF/DEMO: "Let me show you what the PL-300 exam really tests. Open the Microsoft Learn objectives page and you'll see four areas. Prepare the data, model the data, visualize and analyze, and deploy and maintain. It's a good syllabus. It makes you touch Power Query, relationships, DAX, and the service. But look closer at how it's graded. It's multiple choice and a few interactive tasks against clean, tidy sample data. The exam hands you a dataset that already makes sense. Real work never does. In real work the sales table and the finance table disagree, dates are stored as text, and someone renamed a column last Tuesday. The exam doesn't test whether you can untangle that. It tests whether you recognize the right menu."
