CaseWhen · content strategy

The split, the volumes, and the goal.

What we publish, how much of each, and why. Every number below comes from the keyword and cadence plan; the format mix comes from what actually drives engagement, saves, and search rankings for a Power BI audience.

The goal

Three jobs, one funnel

1 · Get found

Rank on Google and get cited by AI

Blog articles answer a real search term in the first lines, with a table and FAQ, so we win the snippet and get quoted by ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity. This is the discovery engine.

2 · Build authority

Founders BI leaders trust

Austin and Saju post opinion and real-release reactions on LinkedIn, backed by YouTube and case studies. This is the layer a referred buyer checks before they call.

3 · Reach & saves

Be seen by everyone who reports a number

Short-form video and carousels reach far beyond people already searching, and earn saves that compound. This is top of funnel.

The funnel: short-form reach → LinkedIn and YouTube authority → blog gets found and converts buyer-intent searches → a booked, fixed-price Reporting Foundation Review.

Blog

~43 articles a month · what they're about

Format mix: ~70% practitioner how-to and direct-answer (the search-harvest base), ~20% buyer-intent money pages (cost, licensing, "vs"), ~10% flagship authority (the governance framework and pillars). English daily to build the search footprint; German three times a week, where a named-author byline is the one thing no local competitor does.

LinkedIn

~26 founder posts a month

Format mix: ~40% plain-text contrarian / opinion (our highest-reach format), ~30% numbered carousel (the save-and-reference engine), ~20% story and real-release reaction, ~10% stat or quote card. Austin posts in English, Saju in German on a different rhythm, so the combined founder feed shows up 5-6× a week.

Short-form video

~30 scripts a month

Format mix: ~40% numbered countdown carousels (built for saves), ~35% mistake-callouts ("are you still doing this?", built for saves and shares), ~25% before/after and relatable moments (built for reach). Each script is built for one job — saves, shares, or reach — because on short-form those come from different content.
